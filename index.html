How it plays

Buy low here. Sell high there.

Every turn is a trip. You see what the next city pays before you leave, so every move is a bet you can plan.

1

Read the report

Tap any city to see its market report. Lobster is cheap in Halifax. New York pays a fortune for it.

2

Fill your bag

Buy what's cheap where you are. Start with a backpack, then upgrade to a van and a box truck.

3

Take the trip

Ride the bus or drive. Pay living costs on the road, get through customs, and deal with whatever happens on the way.

4

Sell and invest

Cash out, pay down your loan, then put your money into stocks, startups and whole companies.

The map

Forty cities. Six continents.

You start on the East Coast with a bus ticket. Grow your net worth and you unlock a cargo plane to London, Tokyo, Lagos and beyond. Every city has its own taste: Toronto pays anything for hockey gear, Dubai loves gold, and Tokyo can't get enough Maine lobster. Tap any product to see where it sells highest next turn, and check Hot Deals for prices that will still be there when you land.

Ways to get rich

Trading is just the start.

Trade

Buy and sell goods

From phone cases to vintage watches. Prices move every week, and big orders move the market.

Stocks

Play the market

52 companies with weekly prices, dividends, crashes and rallies. Unlock short selling once you're rich.

Startups

Back the next big thing

Most fail. Some raise big rounds. A few get bought out and pay you back several times over.

Companies

Own the business

Buy everything from a hot dog cart to an airline. Collect profits every week, sell high, and buy back low.

Bank

Borrow and save

Start with a loan you have to pay back on time. Park your cash in savings to earn interest and keep it safe.

Lifestyle

Spend it like a billionaire

Penthouses, supercars, superyachts, private jets, a private island, even a sports team. Some hold value, some cost a fortune to keep, and fame boosts your companies.

Rich list

Climb the world rich list

Twenty billionaires stand between you and the top spot. Their fortunes move every week. Pass them one by one.

Risky

Cut corners

Smuggle goods past the border, buy stuff that fell off a truck, act on insider tips. It pays, until it doesn't.

Difficulty

Pick how real you want it.

Every mode has the same eight rivals. What changes is how kind the world is to you.

Easy

A generous world. Big price gaps, cheap living, soft bad luck. Exact market reports.

Normal

Balanced. Good deals exist if you look. Market reports are close to accurate.

Hard

Close to real life. Fees on sales, rough market reports, no hints.

Realistic

As real as it gets. Thin margins, real costs, shaky reports. One bad trip can sink you.
